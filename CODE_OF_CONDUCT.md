# Code of Conduct

Ferrum is an open-source project built by and for people who like making things.

We want everyone to feel welcome, regardless of their experience level, background, or how big their contribution is.

## Expected behavior

Please:

- be respectful and constructive
- assume good intentions
- welcome different ideas and approaches
- give useful feedback
- help newcomers learn
- focus on the work, not the person

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, trolling, and other disruptive behavior are not welcome.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainers through the repository's available contact channels.

Reports will be taken seriously and handled as privately as reasonably as possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed to keep the community healthy.

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces and interactions.
